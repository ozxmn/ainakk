Unchecked or Falsely Accused?

AI-generated text detectors, tested on Kazakh. A study by Mansur Ozaman.

One sound, five alphabets

Kazakh has written ң in five scripts.

Runes, the Arabic-script töte jazu, Latin, Cyrillic, and Latin again. The AI detectors schools download were built for English.
